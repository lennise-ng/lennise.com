Hello, I'm Lennise — a Malaysian-born entrepreneur, investor, and someone with an almost unreasonable appetite for curiosity.

I co-founded Borong (formerly Dropee), a YC-backed technology company that started with a fairly simple idea and eventually taught me more about business, people, ambition, and human nature than I ever anticipated. These days, I'm a Managing Partner at Redwood Global Capital, where I spend much of my time exploring emerging technologies, investing in businesses, and working with people who are trying to build something meaningful.

Before all that, there was music. I studied it, still love it, and remain convinced that I have an album in me. Evidence pending.

But I'm also fascinated by things beyond business. Art, history, culture, philosophy, and particularly the people who shape the world we live in. I enjoy understanding why certain ideas survive centuries while others disappear, why some people accomplish extraordinary things, and why human beings continue to make the same mistakes despite having several thousand years of documented examples to learn from.

I suppose that's what connects most of my interests: people, their decisions, and the things they leave behind.

I used to write quite a bit. Then came entrepreneurship, an increasingly crowded calendar, and social media's rather impressive ability to consume an afternoon. So I decided to reclaim a little space to think and write again.

This website isn't meant to be a business playbook, a collection of profound life advice, or an attempt at being a thought leader (heaven knows we have enough of those!). It's simply a place for me to document the things I find interesting, challenge my own assumptions, and occasionally make sense of experiences I've had along the way.

Some thoughts may be well-researched. Others might be half-baked. And I reserve the right to change my mind when presented with better information.

If anything here makes you think, laugh, or strongly disagree, I'd love to hear from you.
