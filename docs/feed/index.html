Follow by RSS

This site has no algorithm, no notifications and no mailing list. If you want to know when a new essay goes up, the old-fashioned way still works best: a feed reader.

A feed reader is an app that checks the sites you choose and shows you anything new, in order, with nothing in between. You subscribe once and the essays come to you. Nobody is counted, tracked or sold anything.

How to subscribe

Install a reader if you don't have one — NetNewsWire on iPhone and Mac, Feeder on Android, or Feedly and Inoreader in any browser. Then add this address:

https://www.lennise.com/feed.xml

Most readers will also find it if you just paste lennise.com.

The feed carries the essays in full, not the short notes in Margins. If you open the feed itself in a browser you'll see a wall of code; that's normal, it's written for the reader app, not for you.
