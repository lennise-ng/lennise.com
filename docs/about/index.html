About

I used to write. Then came social media, the doomscrolling, the cheap dopamine. This is me getting back to it — writing about anything and everything I find interesting.

Mostly that means art and culture, entrepreneurship, and history, and the places where those three run into each other: who paid for the work, why some cities became creative or commercial centres at certain moments and then stopped, what someone actually did day to day and what can be learned from it.
